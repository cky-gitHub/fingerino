## What's new in 0.3.1

Fixes for laptops other than the one Fingerino was made on.

- **A smoother preview.** The camera preview no longer stutters on laptops
  whose camera sends a bigger picture than 1280x720. It's now drawn at the
  preview's own size, about 7 times less work per frame (more with a 1080p
  camera), which also leaves more time for recognising your hand.
- **The preview's labels, arrows and hand skeleton are the right size** on
  every screen and with every camera. They were too small where the camera
  sent a bigger picture, and on high-resolution screens.
- **The Home, Gestures and Settings icons show on every computer.** On some
  Windows 11 laptops they were empty boxes, as were the preview's minimise
  and close buttons. Fingerino now draws them itself instead of relying on a
  Windows icon font.
- Start Fingerino with `--debug` to see the frame rate and the size the
  camera really sends, at the bottom of the preview.
