## What's new in 0.3.0

Fingerino is now a **hand-sign shortcut tool**: make a sign, and it does
something.

- **Make your own signs.** Home › *Record a sign…*: hold any hand shape in
  front of the camera for a few seconds, name it, and choose what it does. No
  code, no training. Only the positions of your finger joints are kept, never
  a picture.
- **Any sign can do anything.** A shortcut, a media key, open an app, a
  website or a file, or run a command you typed yourself. *Try it* lets you
  test an action before you save it.
- **The cursor is optional, and now off by default** (for upgraders too).
  If you used it, switch it back on under Gestures › Pointer.
- **Exit is now crossed index and middle fingers**, one hand, held briefly.
  The old two-hands "X" was hard for the camera to see.
- **A redesigned Home**: light and dark theme, your own pause shortcut,
  Windows text size, an optional hand skeleton, and Quit.
- **Free and Premium.** Free: every built-in sign and one sign of your own
  switched on at a time. Premium: up to 30. Every install starts with 14
  days of Premium; afterwards nothing is deleted, extra signs just switch
  off until you choose. Premium goes on sale soon. Licence keys are checked
  on your computer; Fingerino still makes no network request.
